# Weymark

Weymark, officially Estella VIII, is a small airless dwarf planet in the Belt, close enough to the Caravanserai to serve as a practical next stop but far enough from its glare to have a life of its own. Its name is usually traced to an old survey contraction of "way-mark," though locals pronounce it **WEE-mark** and insist the spelling matters. Early prospectors found little to make anyone rich: workable stone, a few modest metal seams, and a captured moonlet bright enough to become a navigational comfort on approach. The first settlements survived as road stations more than boom towns, with Weymark Town and Low Gauge Mine serving the thin traffic that passed through.

Today Weymark is where Belt road culture meets precision corporate logistics. The Guild uses Nell's Rest as a forgiving first-rendezvous station, maintenance yard, and mechanics hub for Teamsters learning real orbital work after Caravanserai; the cheap rooms, mess tables, and longer-term living are down in Weymark Town. Higher up, Bruckner Weymark Depot handles sealed VHM civilian parts, service kits, calibration cargo, and warranty traffic away from the crowded Highliner docks. Nell's Lantern and Lantern Dock remain small local landmarks, while Old Survey Camp sits mostly abandoned. Weymark is not glamorous, but it is useful: a quiet working world where rookies, clerks, yardhands, and sealed precision crates all learn to share the same traffic pattern.
