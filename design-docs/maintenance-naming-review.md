# Maintenance naming audit and rename proposals

Updated: 2026-10-27 (Pacific)
Status: **Proposal only. No live names, prose, IDs, terms or saves changed.** Audited against `588985d`, before adding this review. Preserve any `zzz` comments verbatim; record resolutions alongside them.

## 1. Findings and recommendation

The problem is broader than four bad names: too many shops sound like rows in a coverage spreadsheet. **26 of the 30 full-service/elite names contain Service, Workshop, Works or Engineering; 55 of 63 professional facilities contain Workshop.** The words themselves are useful; the repeated place-plus-function construction is what makes the network feel interchangeable.

I recommend:
- Remove **“visiting rig” / “visiting-rig” / “visiting rigs”** from current player-facing maintenance copy, including staff titles, summaries and referrals. Usually just **rig**, **repair**, **maintenance** or **customer work** is enough. Do not replace it mechanically with “transient rig” or “guest rig.”
- Give the major shops a mix of trade names, personal names, corporate divisions, craft institutions and named premises. Keep geography in the location/path and capabilities in the directory summary, rather than forcing both into every business name.
- Keep strong existing identities: Bruckner, Serrat, Vilar’s chain, Nell’s Rest, Bir Safa, Coldgate, Red Dust, the Armorial Shipwrights, the Seconds Gate and the High Pennant Shipyard. Diversity does not mean gratuitously renaming every established business.
- Keep military, research and industrial departments functional where that is their identity. Keep informal mechanic-search and emergency-action labels unbranded; no invented residents at abandoned sites.
- Treat names as **trade names for the same existing facilities**, not new economic providers, Houses, manufacturers or service capabilities. Personal-name options use existing characters but do not establish founders, relatives, ownership history or dealership rights. Ferraz/Mendonça are optional eponymic readings; nonpersonal alternatives are supplied.

### The four direct offenders

| Current name | Recommended replacement | Identity |
|---|---|---|
| Anvil Visiting-Rig Works | **Combine Overhaul Depot** | Industrial service department at Anvil |
| Svarog Visiting-Rig Service | **SSC Reconstruction Works** | SSC’s heavy repair operation; tenant builders remain separate |
| Daedalus Visiting-Rig Workshop | **Daedalus Refit Laboratory** | Corporate mechanical/control-system facility |
| Farwatch Visiting-Rig Workshop | **Farwatch Technical Section** | Existing EDF technical staff; contract-only arrival unchanged |

No blanket replacement with “Rig Service”: that would remove the phrase but retain the same naming problem. “Rig” itself remains the correct vehicle term, and the broader word “visiting” is not banned in ordinary descriptions of people or travel.
