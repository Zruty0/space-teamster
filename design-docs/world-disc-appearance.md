## Implementation

`src/world-disc-appearance.ts` exports:

- `drawGaiaDisc(ctx, cx, cy, radius)`: blue ocean, irregular green continents and forest patches, large pale northern/southern caps with blue ice margins.
- `drawCrateredDisc(ctx, cx, cy, radius, tint, profile?)`: reusable basins/crater relief; `tint` is a readonly RGB tuple. Celeste uses its existing `[170, 165, 150]` palette and exact legacy appearance by default. Recoloring does not regenerate geometry. The approved Stage2 registry supplies per-world profiles to13 original rocky bodies plus the separately approved Sahra regolith conversion; ice uses the separate shared painter below, and further families remain gated.
- `drawAuthoredWorldDisc(ctx, bodyId, cx, cy, radius, tint)`: shared opt-in dispatch used by central-body and system/moon-marker rendering in `src/orbital.ts`; returns false without drawing for other IDs.

Normalized geometry is generated once, independently of time/random frame state. Craters foreshorten toward the limb. Tiny markers use fewer features; larger discs add fine coastline/forest/crater detail. Relief shading is fixed illustration, not a sun-tracking terminator. Paint and border are clipped inside the circular silhouette; canvas state is restored. No off-limb bloom.

## Seeded foundation — Stage1, 2026-10-23

`src/cratered-world-geometry.ts` exports `crateredWorldProfile(bodyId, overrides?)`, `crateredWorldGeometry(profile?)`, `resolveCrateredWorldProfile(profile)` and `CELESTE_CRATER_PROFILE`. Profiles derive seeds from stable IDs, not names/time; Celeste retains41873 plus explicit legacy basins. Optional crater count/scale, basin coverage and relief contrast are bounded. Crater and basin random streams are independent; changing density never shuffles the basins or existing crater positions. Geometry/points are deeply immutable and cached in a64-entry LRU, independent of tint/contrast/zoom. Eviction/rebuild yields identical geometry.

Stage1 initially used this machinery only for Celeste; Stage2 supplied explicit frozen profiles for13 approved rocky worlds; approved Sahra follow-upd08aea7 adds a14th without changing those thirteen. Both central and moving-marker paths use the same registry and existing body palettes. `/tests/rocky-world-preview.html` is the live gallery/production-orbital preview. The Stage1 comparison page now identifies its three examples as live and verifies their pixels match the approved demonstrations. [Stage plan and approval status](planet-visual-stages.md).

## Icy family — Stage3, 2026-10-23

`src/icy-world-appearance.ts` exports `drawIcyDisc(ctx, cx, cy, radius, tint, profile)`, dispatched by the same central/marker helper. Seven current profiles: Beira, Macao, Mercy, Teide, Estella XIII/XIV and the rogue/KBO fragment. The original eighth, IX, moved to rocky/regolith rendering as approved Sahra follow-upd08aea7. Pale frost fields, darker old ice and subdued impacts distinguish ice from recolored lunar terrain. Review revision removes fracture geometry/rendering and thin outlines around frost patches, retaining all frost/basin/crater positions and counts. Beira has no exposed ocean; Mercy's original atmosphere is drawn independently by orbital.ts, unchanged. All rim sites remain on solid ground; no placement changes.

`icy-world-geometry.ts`: immutable normalized frost arrays plus reused crater/basin geometry, independent versioned streams from permanent IDs,32-entry LRU. Frost footprint0–1 and dirt0–1 supplement the existing crater/basin/relief controls. The removed fracture stream never affected retained geography. Frost is a dimensionless art control, not measured ice coverage. Prefixes/unrelated layers survive density changes; tint/dirt/contrast/zoom cannot move geometry. Below14px omit impacts;14–59px use the first five impacts; larger discs add remaining impacts. Frost geography is identical at all sizes. No crack strokes at any LOD.

`ESTELLA_ICY_WORLD_TINTS` / `icyWorldTintFor` supply frozen art-only palettes to the common dispatcher: cyan Beira, jade Macao, ivory Mercy, taupe Teide, pearl-blue XIII, violet-grey XIV, ash/sage rogue. Existing `BodyDef.color`, terrain, atmospheric palettes and physics remain unchanged. Standalone `drawIcyDisc` still accepts any tint for the comparison preview. Sahra/IX now has a warm regolith palette and subdued rocky relief over buried ice; its radius/gravity/rotation/orbits and terrain collision remain unchanged. See [Sahra](locations/sahra.md).

Common clip/shade/outline helpers moved unchanged to `world-disc-primitives.ts`; the original independent Celeste/Gaia fixture remains frozen. No changes to rocky-world profiles, Balor, physics or saves. Full scope/review gate in [stage plan](planet-visual-stages.md).
