# Maintenance Stage 4 — full-service anchors

Updated: 2026-10-27 (Pacific)
Status: Stage 4 accepted. This document preserves its copy and rollout snapshot. [Stage 5](maintenance-stage-5.md) is now implemented and awaits final network review.

This stage promotes the remaining **25 full-service anchors**: 24 receive the complete bespoke text below; Cadiz keeps its established identity and five existing prose fields, gains B-quality work and a new in-character referral. Nell’s Rest remains the already implemented pilot. All business names, account IDs, receiving IDs and referral catchments stay unchanged. The new named staff are shop characterization only, not mission contacts or additional economic providers.

The resulting internal mix is **57 basic / 65 professional / 26 full-service / two elite**, plus nine emergency-only sites. These classes are never displayed to players. Rochefort and Svarog stay professional branches with future specialist referrals; their elite upgrades remain Stage 5.

## Terms and boundaries

The 24 standard promotions use B diagnosis / B work, B–D stock, 800 cr/hour, two base inspection hours, 0.9 labor-time multiplier, 1.0 parts multiplier and honesty 1. All four existing installations remain offered. Cadiz changes only its work capability from C to B: diagnosis B, stock C–D, 600 cr/hour, normal time and parts multipliers remain. No new repair mechanism, component, equipment, dealership authority or warranty monopoly.

B work covers 314 of the 320 unique repair faults (513 of 520 component/fault pairs), leaving the six A-only definitions to the elites. Total failures still require replacement. Standardized replacement remains available even when the fault is unidentified. Referrals describe the destination’s capabilities, never diagnose hidden faults. Private Perun servicing remains local after legitimate arrival; no public recommendation leads to Perun.

Existing inspection expiry and unused branch credit rules remain. No save-version or compatibility layer. Emergency restart remains free, local and enabled only by actual damage-caused grounding. All nine emergency-only sites remain unstaffed. No shipping, access, flight, artwork or Stage 5 changes are authorized by this pass.
