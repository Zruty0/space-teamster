# Gaia shipping direction

Updated: 2026-10-23
Status: approved direction; runtime rename/providers/routing not yet implemented.

## Approved decisions

- Rename Gaia Main Customs Station / Main Customs and Arrival Hub to **Richelieu Orbital Terminal**. This is the biggest transit hub in the Estella system. Retain station ID `estella-iii-main-customs-station` and terminal ID `estella-iii-main-customs`.
- Approximately50% of Gaia-bound traffic and50% of Gaia-origin traffic should use Richelieu; the remainder serves surface destinations directly. Apply the direction to freight and passengers, rather than balancing inbound against outbound. Surface distribution behind terminal shipments remains background logistics, not a mandatory second player flight.
- Add two institutional entities for the extra Gaia shipments: **GOV**, the sector government (existing Estella Commonwealth/Council), and **EDF**, the Estella Defence Force. EDF work is mostly personnel transfers.
- The majority of transit should come from other, non-named entities. GOV, EDF and established named corporations are identifiable participants, not the whole civilian economy. Exact percentages beyond the orbital/direct split remain unset.
- No exact new route roster, pay schedule, cargo volumes or institutional contacts have been approved yet. No automatic opening of restricted sites to empty travel.

## Suggested implementation/content direction — not yet approved

- Represent ordinary civilian issuers by descriptive roles (agricultural cooperative, equipment manufacturer, university travel office, passenger booking agency) without inventing named companies or market tags for each. Author routes around actual site roles rather than all-to-all filler.
- GOV: civil-service travel, inspectors, scientific/public-works teams, public equipment and supplies. EDF: postings, relief crews, leave returns, trainees and liaison staff; a smaller supporting freight component.
- Differentiate the cities: Orselle civic/scientific/medical business; Rochefort machinery and precision manufacturing; Valenne food and agricultural supplies; Mirabel resident/visitor/cultural travel; Cousteau import-heavy polar fieldwork and crew rotations.
- Preserve existing named-provider identity and cargo purpose when considering gateway routing. Determine how gateway-only traffic and protected/restricted destinations enter the overall50/50 accounting before implementation.
