Updated: 2026-10-26
Status: Stage 1 comments incorporated; Stage 2 pilot implemented. Final staffed rollout remains staged. See the [specification](maintenance-stage-1-specification.md) and [Stage 2 handoff](maintenance-stage-2.md).

All **159 physical destinations** appear once. The final network has 150 staffed facilities and nine emergency-only sites. During rollout, every location without a staffed facility gets the same free emergency self-help procedure. This does not invent permanent staff at abandoned sites or caches.

Internal class/terms codes are review metadata, never player labels. E0 means emergency self-help; S0/S1/S2/S3 are general defaults; W0 is the Wells basic-mechanic exception; E-* retains an authored business; V2 is the later Cadiz capability upgrade. S0 honesty is 0.95 and W0 honesty is 0.8. Svarog is now elite.

Referrals use approved practical catchments and may name future shops. The normal recommendation is followed by an elite alternative unless the normal destination is already elite. All referral destinations use public entrances; no hidden-site knowledge or landing permissions are granted.

Copy-family labels identify the complete Stage 3 Markdown review workload, not approved prose. Future bespoke sites use the indicated professional family until their own stage. Existing named shops and the Weymark pilot retain separately authored writing. All variants and substitutions must be supplied for comment before Stage 3 rollout.
